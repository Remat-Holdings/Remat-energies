Specialized Gas Turbine Services

The range of gas turbine repair and maintenance solutions we offer includes:

Hot Gas Path (HGP) Inspections
Routine and critical inspections of combustion liners, transition pieces, fuel nozzles, turbine blades, and vanes. Performed to assess thermal wear and material degradation in high-temperature sections.
Combustion Inspections
Focused review of fuel nozzles, flame detectors, crossfire tubes, and combustion liners. Typically performed on a regular maintenance cycle to ensure proper ignition, combustion, and emissions performance.
Borescope Inspections
Non-invasive inspections using high-resolution video scopes to visually assess internal components such as blades, vanes, combustors, and fuel nozzles — without a full disassembly.
Major Inspections / Overhauls
Full teardown inspections of all major turbine sections, including compressor, combustion, and turbine modules. Typically includes split-line opening, rotor inspection, casing evaluation, and alignment checks.
Inlet & Exhaust Inspections
Visual and dimensional inspection of inlet guide vanes (IGVs), inlet filters, expansion joints, exhaust diffusers, and collector systems. Ensures airflow integrity and structural condition.
Generator Inspections
Inspection of generator rotors, stators, windings, insulation systems, and coupling alignment. Often includes EL-CID, wedge tightness, and impedance/resistance testing.
Gearbox Inspections
Inspection of turbine-to-generator gearboxes for tooth wear, alignment, lubrication systems, seals, and thermal behavior. Common in aero-derivative turbine applications.
Starting Package & Auxiliary System Inspections
Evaluation of starters (motor or gas), turning gear systems, lube oil skids, hydraulic systems, and fuel forwarding skids to ensure startup reliability and safe operation.
