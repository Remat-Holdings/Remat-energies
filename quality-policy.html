Quality Policy

Excellence in quality across manufacturing, engineering, and field service.

Our Commitment to Quality

At Remat Energies, quality is built into every part we supply, every repair we perform, and every service we deliver. Our customers depend on mission-critical rotating equipment, and we hold ourselves to the standard that reliability demands.

Remat Energies is committed to providing products and services that meet or exceed customer requirements and applicable statutory and regulatory requirements. We achieve this through skilled people, controlled processes, and a culture of continual improvement across every site and every project.

Our Quality Principles

  • Customer focusUnderstand and meet customer requirements, and deliver on time with OEM or better quality.
  • Safety firstProtect our people, our customers, and the environment in everything we do.
  • Process disciplineFollow documented, controlled processes from engineering and manufacturing through to installation and service.
  • Competent peopleTrain and qualify our team so every job is done right the first time.
  • Continual improvementSet measurable quality objectives, review our performance, and act on what we learn.
  • Trusted suppliersSelect and monitor suppliers who share our commitment to quality.

Commitment to Quality: ISO 9001 Certified Excellence

At Remat Energies, quality isn't just a promise. It's a standard we live by on every project, from engineering and manufacturing to repairs and field service on turbines, compressors, and their associated systems.

Our ISO 9001 certification reflects our commitment to delivering world-class solutions for rotating equipment. It validates our rigorous processes, our precision engineering, and our dedication to customer satisfaction on every project we undertake.
