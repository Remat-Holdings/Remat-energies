Comprehensive Industrial Controls Services for Every Project Phase

Remat delivers end-to-end controls services that keep your systems running at peak performance — from planning to operation, with precision, safety, and quality at every step.

Pre-Commissioning Services

  • Compliance Checks: Confirming systems meet applicable codes, regulations, and safety standards.
  • Instrument Calibration: Fine-tuning instruments for accuracy and consistent performance.
  • Point-by-Point Verification: Confirming communication and function across all control points and interfaces.
  • Pipeline Pressurization & Leak Testing: Verifying system integrity before start-up.
  • Flushing Pipes & Vessels: Removing debris and contaminants to protect equipment.
  • Machine Alignment: Precisely aligning rotating and static equipment.

Commissioning Phase Services for Safe Start-Up & System Validation

Commissioning is where systems come online and performance is validated. Our teams follow structured procedures to bring every system up safely and correctly.

  • Energizing the Control Panel: Safely powering up and verifying panel operation.
  • Instrument Loop Tests: Checking accuracy, calibration, and connectivity between field instruments and the control system.
  • Functional Tests: Validating control logic, alarms, and interlocks.
  • Network Check: Testing communication network stability and data transfer.
  • Engine Running Tests: Live runs to confirm performance under real operating conditions.

The result is a fully tested, optimized, and reliable system ready for steady-state operation.

Start-Up Phase Services for System Start, Loading & Performance Testing

Start-up brings your systems into full operation. We manage every step with a focus on safety and peak performance.

  • Feeding Systems: Introducing fuel, utilities, and process streams step by step.
  • First Fire: Initiating ignition under strict safety protocols.
  • Loading Machines: Gradually applying load to test stability and response.
  • Reliability Test (72-Hour Run): Continuous monitoring to confirm dependable operation.
  • Performance Test: Measuring efficiency and output against design specifications.

You gain confidence that your system is validated and ready for long-term operation.

Operation & Maintenance Services for Lifecycle Support and System Stability

Our support continues long after start-up, keeping your control systems efficient throughout their lifecycle.

  • Troubleshooting: Rapid diagnosis and restoration of system function.
  • Revamping: Modernizing existing systems to current standards.
  • Updates & Upgrades: Adopting newer technology for performance, scalability, and compliance.
  • Operation & Maintenance: Ongoing technical support, monitoring, and routine maintenance.
  • Replacement: Swapping obsolete or underperforming components.
  • Specialized Engineering: Tailored solutions for complex requirements.

Remat is a committed partner for maximizing uptime, extending equipment life, and keeping daily operation consistent.
