About Us

We are a premier global service and technology partner focusing on carbon footprint reduction, energy security, and value creation.

We are your trusted partner in mission-critical equipment, with decades of expertise in designing, manufacturing, and servicing rotating equipment to keep your plant running efficiently and reliably.

Remat Energies is an energy-efficient technology company delivering advanced rotating equipment solutions to industries like oil and gas, power generation, and petrochemicals. Our solutions help reduce carbon emissions and enhance plant reliability for operators worldwide, with a steadfast commitment to safety and global service excellence.

Our Vision

To become the premier global service and technology partner with a clear focus on carbon footprint reduction, energy security, and value creation.

Our Mission

Our mission is to keep turbomachinery and their associated systems running reliably and optimally.

We create value for customers by minimizing emissions and enhancing revenue.

We accomplish our mission by providing:

  • Traditional aftermarket services, with OEM or better-quality parts and repairs, faster response, and the most qualified field service organization in the industry.
  • Best-in-class revamp technologies, control systems, and turnkey solutions with shorter cycle times.
  • Turbine/engine drive conversions to e-drives.

A Message from Our CEO

Welcome to Remat Energies. We are committed to building a sustainable, energy-secure future through innovative, energy-efficient rotating equipment solutions. Serving industries like oil and gas, power generation, and petrochemicals, our mission is to help clients reduce carbon emissions, enhance operational reliability, and drive measurable value. With hundreds of years of combined expertise, our team of skilled professionals provides end-to-end services, from design and manufacturing to installation, maintenance, and overhauls.

Our commitment to quality, safety, and environmental responsibility drives everything we do. At Remat Energies, we believe that technology and sustainability go hand in hand, and we are proud to lead the way in reducing the global carbon footprint of industrial operations. Thank you for your interest in our company. We look forward to creating a cleaner, more resilient future together.
