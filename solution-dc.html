Advanced Compressor Technology Driving the Energy Transition

Our novel 7 Degree of Freedom™ (7 DoF™) impellers provide high efficiency, wide operating range, and superior flow capacity, allowing compressor operators to maximize the environmental and economic performance of existing assets. Our advanced impeller technology for compressors is designed to meet the evolving demands of the energy transition. As compressors play a critical role in enabling technologies like carbon capture, utilization, and storage (CCUS), as well as hydrogen as a clean energy source, Remat is at the forefront of innovation. By optimizing performance and improving efficiency, our impeller solutions support the global shift toward sustainable energy while reducing emissions and enhancing reliability in critical applications.
