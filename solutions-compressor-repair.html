Full-Scope Compressor Repair Built Around Your Outage Window

Remat Energies repairs centrifugal, reciprocating, and screw compressors across the full spectrum of severity, from routine wear and seal replacement to major casing damage and rotor failure. Every job begins with a thorough teardown inspection so the repair plan is based on what we actually find, not assumptions.

Our shop and field teams work from decades of turbomachinery experience, drawing on in-house machining, balancing, and non-destructive testing to return your unit to OEM tolerances. Whether you need a fast turnaround on a single component or a coordinated overhaul during a planned shutdown, Remat delivers repairs that hold up in service.

Component-Level Repair for Rotating and Reciprocating Equipment

Remat specializes in repairing the components that keep centrifugal and reciprocating compressors running: rotors, impellers, diaphragms, seals, bearings, and casings. Critical repairs are our specialty, whether that means one technician re-machining a single seal face or a full crew rebuilding a multi-stage rotor during a plant outage.
