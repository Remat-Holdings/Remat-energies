Rotolign – Laser Shaft Alignment

Rotolign is a laser shaft alignment system designed to precisely align rotating machinery and minimize mechanical stress. Proper alignment extends equipment life and improves operational efficiency.

  • Reduces excessive vibration
  • Prevents premature bearing and seal wear
  • Minimizes coupling failures and energy losses

Common Use Cases

  • Pumps, compressors, turbines, and motors
  • Commissioning and post-maintenance verification
  • Reliability improvement programs
  • Preventive maintenance programs
