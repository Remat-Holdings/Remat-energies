Our Centrifugal Compressor Revamp & Repair Services

Remat Energies provides centrifugal compressor revamp solutions to retrofit our industry-leading impeller technology into the existing compressor case of any OEM. Our novel 7 Degree of Freedom™ (7 DoF™) impellers provide high efficiency, wide operating range, and superior flow capacity, allowing compressor operators to maximize the environmental and economic performance of existing assets.

Compressor revamps restore aging equipment to peak efficiency, optimizing performance for current and future operational demands. By upgrading to modern, high-efficiency impellers, you can reduce power consumption, lower operational costs, and minimize your carbon footprint. Revamps address common issues like high vibration, poor rotordynamic stability, and recurring surge. Achieve sustainability goals while cutting CO2 emissions and saving on energy costs.

Why Revamp a Compressor?

Increased Efficiency

Our modern, high-efficiency impellers reduce the power consumption of centrifugal compressors, yielding lower operational costs and reducing carbon footprint.

New Process Conditions

As your operations change over time, many compressors operate far from their original design point. Revamps provide a means to optimize aging compressors for present and future needs.

Solve Reliability Issues

Our impeller technology can solve field issues, such a high vibration, poor rotordynamic stability, or recurring surge.

Sustainability Goals

Reducing the power consumption and carbon footprint of your compressor operations can help you achieve your sustainability goals – including reducing CO2 taxes.
