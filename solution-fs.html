How We Deliver – “Turnkey Filtration”

Remat executes complete filtration and oil-conditioning projects — from assessment to commissioning and lifecycle support — through a clearly defined process:

  • Assess: We evaluate process conditions, flow ranges, contaminants, and ΔP limits to define the filtration strategy.
  • Engineer: Remat designs the full system — media type, vessel code, material selection, layout, and tie-ins — aligned with client specifications and site constraints.
  • Supply: All components are sourced and manufactured under a global quality framework, with controlled production and logistics.
  • Install & Commission: Our field engineers handle on-site assembly, leak testing, baseline DP setup, and complete SAT certification.
  • Sustain: We ensure long-term performance through OEM spares, change-out programs, KPI monitoring, and reliability optimization reviews.

Oil Cleanliness & High-Velocity Oil Flush (HVOF) Services

Contamination control is at the core of Remat's filtration philosophy. We help operators meet and maintain ISO 4406:2021 cleanliness codes, protecting critical components and extending lubricant life.

Oil Cleanliness Guide – ISO 4406:2021

Dirty Unfiltered

  • ISO Code: 23/21/18
  • Particles > 4µm: 40,000
  • Typical Condition: Dirty / Unfiltered
  • Application Example: New oil as received

Moderate

  • ISO Code: 20/18/15
  • Particles > 4µm: 5,000
  • Typical Condition: Moderate
  • Application Example: Gearboxes, hydraulics

Good

  • ISO Code: 18/16/13
  • Particles > 4µm: 1,300
  • Typical Condition: Good
  • Application Example: Turbines, compressors

Very Clean

  • ISO Code: 17/15/12
  • Particles > 4µm: 640
  • Typical Condition: Very Clean
  • Application Example: Servo valves, actuators

High Precision

  • ISO Code: 16/14/11
  • Particles > 4µm: 320
  • Typical Condition: High Precision
  • Application Example: Bearing header oil

Ultra Clean

  • ISO Code: 15/13/10
  • Particles > 4µm: 160
  • Typical Condition: Ultra Clean
  • Application Example: Aerospace, nuclear

High-Velocity Oil Flush (HVOF)

Remat and its field partners provide HVOF commissioning and maintenance services, ensuring new or refurbished oil systems achieve target cleanliness before operation.

HVOF removes debris, varnish, and particulate contamination, validating system integrity before startup — a crucial step for turbines, compressors, and hydraulic systems.
