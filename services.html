Engineering Services to Extend Equipment Life

Performance Analysis

We evaluate compressor, pump, and turbine performance against original design curves to identify efficiency losses and recommend corrective action before they impact output.

Rotordynamics Analysis

Lateral and torsional rotordynamic studies identify critical speeds and instability risks, so rotating assemblies run smoothly across the full operating range.

Structural (FEA) Analysis

Finite element analysis pinpoints stress concentrations and fatigue risk in castings, housings, and rotating components, guiding design changes before failure occurs.

Noise & Vibration Analysis

Acoustic and vibration surveys diagnose the root cause of abnormal noise or vibration, protecting equipment and helping plants meet workplace noise regulations.

Machinery Design

Our engineers design and modify rotating equipment components, from seals to impellers, tailored to your operating conditions and reliability targets.

Proposal & Bid Evaluation

We provide independent technical review of vendor proposals and bids, helping you compare options confidently and avoid costly scope gaps.

Computational Fluid Dynamics

CFD modeling reveals flow behavior inside compressors, turbines, and piping systems, supporting aero path revamps and troubleshooting efficiency losses.

Root Cause Failure Analysis

When equipment fails, our engineers investigate metallurgy, operating history, and design records to find the true cause and prevent repeat failures.

Retrofit & Upgrade Engineering

We engineer upgrades such as gas seal conversions and aero path revamps that improve reliability and efficiency without a full equipment replacement.

Field Engineering Support

Our engineers support field teams on-site during turnarounds and outages, providing real-time technical decisions when issues arise.
