Comprehensive Repair Services for Mission-Critical Equipment

At Remat Energies, we specialize in comprehensive overhauls for mission-critical turbomachinery. Our repair services cover every step, from disassembly and cleaning to inspection, machining, rotor balancing, welding, coatings, and painting. We expertly service a wide range of equipment, including centrifugal compressors (API 617), steam turbines (API 611, API 612), pumps (API 610), blowers, fans, gearboxes, generators, and extruders.

With precision craftsmanship and a commitment to excellence, we ensure your equipment operates reliably and efficiently, minimizing downtime and maximizing performance.
