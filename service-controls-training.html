Remat Controls Training

Remat Energies offers OEM-independent training programs designed to strengthen the knowledge and skills of plant operators, engineers, and maintenance teams.

Our training covers every stage of system operation — from configuration and troubleshooting to optimization — across major control platforms, including GE, Siemens, Allen-Bradley, and others.

Each program is customized to your plant's specific control architecture, providing hands-on, practical instruction that improves operational reliability and safety and minimizes downtime.

Training & Competence Enhancement

Remat helps clients strengthen their teams through specialized controls and rotating equipment training. Our programs are designed for professionals in instrumentation, electrical, and mechanical disciplines, so they gain the skills needed for safe, efficient, and reliable operations.

Led by experienced instructors, training can be held at Remat facilities or on-site at customer locations, combining classroom learning with real-world applications such as installation, commissioning, and start-up of rotating equipment and process plants.

Training areas include:

  • Controllers & Automation Systems: Siemens Step7 & WinCC, GE RX3i & Cimplicity, Rockwell ControlLogix & FactoryTalk, Woodward MicroNet & Cimplicity.
  • Dedicated Controllers: Bently Nevada 3500, CCC Series 3++, GE Speedtronic Mark VIe, Triconex / Trident.
  • Rotating Equipment: Practical training for onshore and offshore machinery used in oil & gas, petrochemical, and power industries.

Remat training programs build technical confidence and ensure your teams are ready to operate and maintain critical systems with precision.

Controls and Industry Training

Remat offers a complete portfolio of technical and operational training programs designed to meet the needs of our customers worldwide.

Our highly qualified instructors deliver comprehensive, hands-on courses aligned with recognized industry standards, so participants gain both practical skills and an in-depth understanding of control systems, automation, and industrial processes.

Training sessions are available at our dedicated Remat facilities or directly at customer sites, offering maximum flexibility for your team's development.

Through our Training Academy, we run an ongoing program that prepares customer field service engineers for the installation, commissioning, and start-up of rotating equipment and process plants — empowering them with the expertise needed to work safely and efficiently.
