Remat Tilt-Pad Journal Bearings for Critical Industrial Applications

About Remat Bearing Services

Remat Energies brings decades of expertise in inspecting, repairing, and manufacturing tilt-pad, sleeve, thrust, and spherical bearings. Our tilt-pad journal bearings improve turbomachinery performance with standard and custom designs. We reverse-engineer existing bearings to deliver brand-new replacements, using in-house centrifugally cast ASTM-B23 Grade 2 virgin babbitt.

  • Standards: Designed to meet current API vibration standards

Services

  • Reverse engineering for all-new replacement bearings
  • New tilt-pad journal bearings in standard or custom designs
  • Bearing and rotordynamic troubleshooting by our engineering team

Applications

  • Steam and gas turbines
  • Pumps and gearboxes
  • Generators and other high-speed turbomachinery

Our Advantage — Custom-Built Bearings for Tough Conditions

Remat bearings deliver a competitive edge by meeting today's strict API vibration standards with precision-engineered rotor and bearing systems. When standard catalog bearings fall short, we provide custom solutions with tailored clearances, preloads, and journal diameters.

Our optimized lubrication reduces power loss and pad temperature, while compact designs combine thrust and journal bearings for efficiency. Built from advanced materials, our bearings withstand high speeds, heavy loads, extreme temperatures, and specialized lubricants, ensuring peak performance in the most demanding applications.
